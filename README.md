# Analysis of Water Insecurity Across the United States

## Overview
This project visualizes data from the 2025 TidyTuesday dataset on water insecurity and adds social and economic parameters to produce a county by county analysis of associations between rates of water insecurity and socio-economic factors. Water insecurity data was obtained from the [January 28, 2025 TidyTuesday dataset](https://github.com/rfordatascience/tidytuesday/blob/main/data/2025/2025-01-28/readme.md). Social, economic, and regional data was obtained or derived from the [U.S. Census Bureau Data API](https://www.census.gov/data/developers.html). For this project, we will be defining water insecurity as the percentage of households within a county that do not have access to full plumbing.

## Research Questions
This project seeks to answer two questions with out available data:
1. What demographic and economic characteristics are associated with higher and lower rates of water insecurity?

2. Which regions saw the biggest changes in water insecurity rates from 2022 to 2023? Did rates increase or decrease in those regions? Are county demographic, regional, or economic characteristics associated with these changes in water insecurity rates?

These questions help us explain what regions of the country and which communities are most disproportionately impacted by access to household water.
